# Candoux 4 design system

Candoux 4 uses the split hero, floating information card, warm stone backgrounds and ruled feature grid of [Rulebase](https://www.landingfolio.com/inspiration/post/rulebase). [Epidemic Sound](https://www.landingfolio.com/inspiration/post/epidemic-sound) informs the italic display type, pill navigation, dense photo cards, saturated chapter backgrounds and stepped booking panel. The photographs and information describe the apartment and Mount Buller.

| Role | Choice |
| --- | --- |
| Paper and stone | `#f3f1ed` for reading surfaces and `#e9e4dd` for information panels |
| Ink and charcoal | `#1a1917` for the split hero and footer; `#27272c` for the gallery |
| Color chapters | Cyan `#5dcff3` for winter, yellow `#ffda47` for summer and the illustrated journey, pink `#ff85c4` for booking |
| Type | Fraunces for display headings and deliberate italic phrases; DM Sans for controls, prose and labels |
| Layout | Centered shell up to 1320px, fluid symmetric gutters, generous chapter spacing, split grids that stack on small screens |
| Shape | Small corner radius on media and facts; full pills for actions and compact navigation; square steps behind the booking callout |

Use flat surfaces and fine rules to organize practical details. Keep text ink-colored on saturated backgrounds and near-white on dark surfaces. Give interactive targets at least 44px height, keep visible keyboard focus, and respect reduced motion. The menu and gallery are native dialogs; the season choices use pressed states and the FAQ uses native disclosures. Preserve the existing property booking, contact, photography credits and resort links.
